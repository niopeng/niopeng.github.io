Shichong (Nio) Peng

PhD Candidate · 3D Vision & Learned Dynamics

I build learned neural simulators of dynamic visual content — explicit 3D representations (neural point clouds, 3D Gaussians) reconstructed from real images and rolled forward by learned dynamics models, so that captured scenes and subjects can be rendered, edited, and animated rather than just replayed.

I'm a PhD candidate in Computer Science at Simon Fraser University, advised by Ke Li. Most recently I was a Research Scientist Intern with Meta's Codec Avatars group; earlier in my PhD I worked on neural rendering and the IMLE family of generative models. Most people call me Nio. I'm currently on the research job market.
